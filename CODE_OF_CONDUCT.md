# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(© Contributor Covenant, CC BY 4.0).

In short: be respectful and kind, welcome people of every background and experience level, give and take feedback
gracefully, and no harassment, personal attacks or discrimination. Maintainers may remove comments, issues and
contributions that break this, and may ban repeat offenders.

Report concerns to the maintainer at anand34577@gmail.com. Reports are handled privately.
